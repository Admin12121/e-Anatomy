<section>
  <h2>About Voxel Anatomy</h2>
  <p>Voxel Anatomy is an interactive anatomy learning atlas for exploring radiology slices, anatomical areas, and guided medical learning modules.</p>
</section>

<section>
  <h2>Explore Anatomy Visually</h2>
  <p>The platform brings anatomy and radiology into an interactive environment where learners can explore anatomical regions, inspect medical imaging views, and move between visual learning experiences without leaving the atlas.</p>
</section>

<section>
  <h2>Built for Learning</h2>
  <p>Voxel Anatomy is designed to make complex anatomical structures easier to inspect and understand through focused, interactive visual tools.</p>
</section>
